# WeaveDoc Trademark Policy

**The code is open; the name is not a free-for-all.** WeaveDoc is released under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) — you may use, modify, redistribute, and build commercial things on it. That license deliberately grants **no trademark rights** (Apache-2.0 §6). This document says what you may and may not do with the *name*.

"WeaveDoc", the `weavedoc` wordmark, and any WeaveDoc logo are trademarks of **Sooho Choi**.

The goal is narrow and, we hope, uncontroversial: **nobody should have to guess whether something is the official WeaveDoc.** Nothing here is meant to restrict your use of the software itself.

## What you may do — no permission needed

- **Use, modify, fork, and redistribute the software** under Apache-2.0, including commercially.
- **Use the name functionally.** WeaveDoc's design bakes the name into functional identifiers, and those stay usable in any fork or derivative: the `.weavedoc/` runtime directory, the `weavedoc-*` skill names, the `weavedoc` CLI command, config keys, file formats, and documentation of any of the above. A fork that must rename its own directories would not be a real fork, and that is not the intent of this policy.
- **State facts about origin or compatibility.** "Based on WeaveDoc", "a WeaveDoc fork", "compatible with WeaveDoc", "reads WeaveDoc mines", "we use WeaveDoc internally" — all fine, as long as they are true and do not imply that the project endorses or maintains you.
- **Refer to WeaveDoc** in articles, talks, reviews, comparisons, courses, and academic work. Criticism included.
- **Redistribute WeaveDoc unmodified** under its own name, with LICENSE and NOTICE intact.

## What needs permission

- **Naming a product, service, distribution, company, or paid offering "WeaveDoc"** — or a name confusingly close to it (e.g. "WeaveDocs", "WeaveDoc Pro", "WeaveDoc Cloud"). Name your fork or product something of your own; then say it is based on WeaveDoc.
- **Branding a modified version as WeaveDoc** in a way that suggests it is the official release. If you ship changed behavior, make the distinction visible.
- **Domain names, social accounts, app-store listings, or package names** that read as the official WeaveDoc.
- **Logos and visual identity**, and any use implying sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or certification.
- **Registering "WeaveDoc" (or a confusingly similar mark) as a trademark** in any jurisdiction.

## The short version

> Take the code and do what you like with it. Don't take the name in a way that makes someone think your thing is the official WeaveDoc — and if you need the name to point to a file path or to state a fact, that is already allowed.

## Asking

Anything not covered here, or a use you would like permission for: open an issue at <https://github.com/starsh2001/weavedoc/issues>. Reasonable requests are usually fine; asking costs nothing.

This policy may be updated; the version in the repository at the time of your use is the one that applies.
